FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Viyo?

Viyo is a creator-focused video platform where people watch short drama series and creators' videos, and creators can post their work and use AI tools, including one that turns a long video into ready-to-post short clips.

Is Viyo free?

Yes, Viyo is free to join and use. Watching the feed, posting, messaging, daily check-ins and missions cost nothing. Some AI tools and later drama episodes use Viyo Coins, which you can earn through check-ins and missions or buy in coin packs. Viyo Premium is an optional subscription for unlimited drama episodes.

Where can I use Viyo?

Viyo runs in your web browser on phones and computers at https://www.viyo.site/app/. You can add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

What does the AI Repurposer do?

You upload a longer video, such as a talk, interview, podcast or tutorial. Viyo transcribes it, works out what the whole video is about, finds the moments that make sense on their own, and turns up to 6 of them into vertical 9:16 clips with burned-in captions and a short hook line. Each clip is checked automatically for framing, sound level and caption timing before you see it.

How long can the video be, and how long are the clips?

Videos up to about half an hour work. Clips are between about 12 and 60 seconds. You can choose Auto, Short (about 15 to 30 seconds, 40 at most), Medium (about 30 to 45 seconds, 55 at most) or Long (about 45 to 60 seconds). Only a standout moment may run past the Short or Medium limit, and no clip is cut before its point.

What does the AI Repurposer cost?

Your first video is free (up to 10 minutes long). After that it costs 40 Viyo Coins per video. If no clip comes out good enough to publish, the coins are refunded automatically.

Does it work on videos without talking?

Yes. For videos with little or no speech, such as cooking or travel footage with music, Viyo picks clips from the picture instead, and you can describe the look you want in a sentence.

Will Viyo make up clips if my video has no good moments?

No. If no moment is strong enough to stand on its own, Viyo tells you so and refunds your coins rather than giving you weak clips.

What are short dramas on Viyo?

Short dramas are series told in short episodes. On Viyo the first 3 episodes of a series are free. Later episodes are unlocked with Viyo Coins, or watched without coins with a Viyo Premium subscription. You can save series to a watchlist.

Can I publish my own short drama?

Yes. Creators can upload episodes to a series from the Create menu.

How do I earn Viyo Coins?

Through daily check-ins, streak bonuses and missions in the app. You can also buy coin packs on the web, from 100 coins for $0.99 to 3,000 coins for $19.99.

Do I need an account?

You can browse as a guest. You need a free account to post, message, buy coins or use the AI tools.

Is my content used to train AI?

No. Viyo does not sell your content and does not use your uploaded photos or videos to train AI models. When you use an AI tool, the content you send to it is processed by an AI provider (OpenAI) to produce the result.

Is private content kept private?

Videos you upload to the AI Repurposer are stored privately and deleted from the processing area after about a day; the finished clips are yours to post or download. You can delete your account and its data from Settings at any time.

What is not available yet?

Glow Up hairstyle previews, where you would see a picture of yourself with a new hairstyle, are still in development. Glow Up text suggestions are available now.

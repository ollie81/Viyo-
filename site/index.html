How publishing works

One long video in. Short clips out.

0:00your video30:00
  1. Upload a talk, interview, podcast or tutorial, up to about half an hour.
  2. Viyo understands the whole video: what it is about, who is speaking, and where each thought starts and ends.
  3. It picks moments that stand on their own and cuts each one on a complete sentence.
  4. Each clip is edited to 9:16 with captions and a hook line, then checked for framing, sound and caption timing.
  5. You watch, download or post the clips you like.

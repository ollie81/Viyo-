Short dramas

Short drama series, one quick episode at a time

Short dramas are stories told in short episodes made for watching on your phone. On Viyo you can browse series, watch the first episodes free, and follow the ones you like.

Watch on Viyo

Watching

  • First 3 episodes free in every series.
  • Later episodes are unlocked with Viyo Coins, or watched without coins with Viyo Premium.
  • Browse by type (short drama, series, movie, short film), by New, Popular or Hot, and by genre such as drama, revenge and romance.
  • Watchlist to save series for later.

Publish your own

Anyone can bring their work to Viyo: short dramas, series, movies, short films and AI films. You set the coin price, keep 65% of the coins from unlocks, and can boost a title so more viewers find it. How publishing works.

Coins and Premium

Coins come from daily check-ins and missions, or coin packs from $0.99. Viyo Premium is an optional weekly, monthly or yearly subscription for unlimited episodes.

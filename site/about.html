About Viyo

Viyo is a creator-focused video platform where people watch short drama series and creators' videos, and creators can post their work and use AI tools, including one that turns a long video into ready-to-post short clips.

Why Viyo exists

Most creators record far more than they can edit. A one-hour conversation might hold five great moments, but finding and cutting them takes hours. Viyo was built so creators can spend their time making things, while the app handles finding the moments, cutting them into shorts and helping with the words around them. Alongside the tools, Viyo is a place to watch: short drama series and creator videos in one feed.

What Viyo is today

  • A web app for phones and computers, at https://www.viyo.site/app/.
  • Short drama series with free first episodes, and a feed of creator videos, photos and posts.
  • AI tools for creators, led by the AI Repurposer, which turns long videos into short clips.
  • Viyo Coins, earned in the app or bought, for AI tools, drama episodes and boosts.

How Viyo treats your content

  • Viyo does not sell your content or conversations and does not use your photos or videos to train AI models.
  • When you use an AI tool, what you send to it is processed by OpenAI to produce the result.
  • You can delete your account and its data from Settings.

Publish on Viyo

Bring your drama, series, movie or film to Viyo

Made a short drama, a web series, a movie or a film? Publish it on Viyo, set your own price, and earn coins every time a viewer unlocks it.

Publish your first title

Free to publish. In the app: tap + then Upload Drama, Movie or Film.

What you can publish

  • Short dramas and series, published episode by episode. Viewers watch the first 3 episodes free, then unlock the rest.
  • Movies and short films, as a single video, priced from the first watch.
  • AI films, made with AI tools, listed in their own category.
  • Vertical or landscape, with your own title, genre, cover image and description.

How you earn

  1. You set the price in Viyo Coins, per episode or for the whole movie.
  2. Viewers unlock your episodes or movie with coins.
  3. You keep 65% of the coins they bought and spend on your title, added to your Wallet in dollars. Each earned coin is worth $0.006. Free coins from check-ins, missions and ads don't count.
  4. Withdraw from $10 to PayPal or mobile money (MTN, Airtel and others). For now the Viyo team sends payouts by hand.

Viewers with Viyo Premium can watch episodes without spending coins.

Getting viewers

  • Your title appears in the Dramas tab, where viewers browse by type, genre and New, Popular or Hot.
  • Boost Series (60 coins) puts your title in front of more viewers.
  • Every episode and post has its own share link with a proper preview for WhatsApp, X and Facebook.
  • Use the AI Repurposer to cut trailers and highlight clips for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
  • Series analytics show how each episode is doing.

What you need

  • A free Viyo account.
  • The rights to everything you upload. Don't upload work that belongs to someone else.

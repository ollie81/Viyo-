Delete your Viyo account

You can delete your account yourself in the app, or ask us to do it.

In the app

  1. Open Viyo and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

Email olli1234x@gmail.com from the address on your account (or tell us your username) with the subject "Delete my Viyo account". We reply within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, posts, photos and videos, Viyo Studio projects and films, AI Coach and Glow Up history, likes, comments and sign-in are permanently deleted. Unused coins and Studio credits are lost.

What we keep

Purchase and payout records that the law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires, and nothing else. Copies you downloaded or posted on other websites are yours to manage.

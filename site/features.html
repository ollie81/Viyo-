Features

Everything you can do on Viyo today

This page lists what is available now. Anything still in development is marked as such.

Watch

  • Short drama series. Browse by type (short drama, series, movie, short film), by New, Popular or Hot, and by genre. The first 3 episodes of a series are free.
  • Watchlist. Save series to come back to.
  • Feed. For You, Videos and Following tabs with videos, photos and text posts from creators.
  • Discover. Search for creators and see new series and trending dramas.

Create and connect

  • Post text, photos, or videos up to 40 minutes.
  • Publish short drama episodes to a series.
  • Likes, comments, follows and direct messages.
  • Coin gifts to creators you like.
  • Boosts: Boost Post (30 coins), Discover Spotlight (25 coins) and Boost Series (60 coins) give your work extra visibility on Viyo.
  • Leaderboard and invites. See top creators and invite friends.

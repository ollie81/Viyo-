Make more from every video you film

Viyo gives creators a place to post and tools that do the slow parts: finding the best moments in a long video, cutting them into shorts, and helping with hooks and captions.

Start creating

What you can do

  • Turn long videos into shorts. The AI Repurposer finds up to 6 moments that work on their own and edits each into a vertical clip with captions and a hook. How it works.
  • Post anything. Videos up to 40 minutes, photos and text posts.
  • Publish a short drama. Add episodes to a series; viewers get the first 3 free.
  • Write better hooks and captions. Hook Check, Caption Ideas and Improve with AI each have one free use a day.
  • Learn from your numbers. Why This Worked explains a post's performance from its real stats, and a weekly report card arrives automatically.
  • Get noticed. Boost a post or a series, or appear in Discover Spotlight, using coins.
  • Earn support. Viewers can send you coin gifts.

Who it suits

Podcasters, educators, coaches, interviewers and anyone who records long-form talking videos and wants short clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or Viyo itself, plus short drama makers looking for an audience that watches series episode by episode.

What it costs

Joining and posting are free. Your first AI Repurposer video is free (up to 10 minutes), then 40 coins per video, refunded automatically if no clip is good enough to publish. See all prices.
